Reyita: The Life of a Black Cuban Woman in the Twentieth Century

I am Reyita, a regular, ordinary person. A natural person, respectful, helpful, decent, affectionate and very independent.

For my mother, it was an embarrassment, that I - of her four daughters - was the only black one. I always felt the difference between us, because she didn't have as much affection for me as she did for my sisters.

Written by María de los Reyes Castillo Bueno + Daisy Rubiera Castillo + Elizabeth Dore // Published by Duke University Press in 2000 // First published in 2000.0 // Retrieved by Internet Archive

Sobre Black Latin American Writers in Translation

Black Latin American Writers in Translation es un recurso para lectores, escritores, editores, estudiantes y profesores interesados en la cultura escrita venezolana en particular y latinoamericana en general. Reúne portadas, metadatos y descargas digitales de 115 obras de autores venezolanos digitalizadas en bibliotecas de Europa y las Américas.
